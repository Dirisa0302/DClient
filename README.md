DClient

«DClient is a project based on WClient, redesigned and modified with a focus on simplicity, lightweight performance, and a cleaner user experience.»

- Afraid your friends will catch you using cheats?
- Afraid they won't want to play with you anymore?

Don't worry! DClient is here.

DClient is a lightweight proxy client with no ClickGUI, keeping everything clean and minimal. It even disguises the proxy as "The Hive" for a more seamless experience.

Why choose DClient?

- No visible cheat UI.
- Minimal and lightweight.
- Legit-looking modules.
- Simple command-based controls.
- Designed for a clean gameplay experience.

Credits

DClient is based on WClient and continues its development with modifications, improvements, and a different design philosophy focused on a lightweight, command-driven experience.

And most importantly...

DClient is good for your health!

Why?

Because with no obvious cheat UI and only legit-looking modules, you're much less likely to get caught by your friends... which means you're also much less likely to experience any real-life physical consequences after the game. 😄
